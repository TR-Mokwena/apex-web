\section{Overview}
Apex is the engineering, sprint and contribution tracker at the centre of the
Eclipse Softworks suite. The most valuable integrations are not bolted on beside
Apex; they \emph{plug into an Apex surface that already exists} --- Tasks,
Contribution Intelligence, the Knowledge Hub, Notifications, Billing or the
Calendar --- so each new product deepens Apex while standing on its own.

Two design principles guide the candidates below:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Plug into a surface.} Every integration reads from or writes to an existing Apex object (a task, a contribution event, an invoice, an incident).
  \item \textbf{Lean South African.} Currency, VAT, POPIA compliance, local payment rails and SA mobile networks are differentiators a generic competitor cannot easily match.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{The suite picture.}
\begin{center}
\textbf{Apex} (do the work) $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Eclipse Bill} (get paid for it)
$\rightarrow$ \textbf{OmniConnect SA} (talk to customers) $\rightarrow$
\textbf{Eclipse Status} (handle incidents).
\end{center}
Each piece feeds the next, and each appears as a natural integration card in Apex.

\section{Live integration}
\subsection{OmniConnect SA \hfill\small\color{muted}(shipped)}
Unified SA customer-communication hub --- SMS, WhatsApp and email with POPIA
compliance and coverage of all four networks.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \hook{Notifications.} Positioned as a \emph{delivery channel}: Apex alerts (risk warnings, milestone updates, mentions) are sent over WhatsApp/SMS via OmniConnect. Surfaced in Integrations (Communication) and as a channel in Notification preferences.
  \item \fit{Apex already models email / push / SMS / in-app channels; OmniConnect is the SA-aware backend for them.}
\end{itemize}

\section{Tier 1 --- highest synergy}

\subsection{Eclipse Bill --- time tracking \& client invoicing}
Track billable time against Apex tasks and projects, then generate client
invoices automatically.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \hook{Tasks/Projects $\rightarrow$ Billing.} Reuses the existing ZAR/VAT invoice + PDF generation already in Apex's billing module.
  \item \fit{Apex already records the work; this is the obvious step that turns tracked effort into revenue.}
  \item \sa{ZAR pricing, 15\% VAT tax invoices, local banking references.}
  \item \effort{Medium. Timer + reporting layer on top of existing task and invoice infrastructure.}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Eclipse Status --- status page \& incident management}
Public status page plus internal incident tracking and post-mortems.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \hook{Tasks + Knowledge Hub + OmniConnect.} Incidents spawn Apex tasks; post-mortems land in the Knowledge Hub; customers receive SMS/WhatsApp updates through OmniConnect.
  \item \fit{Uses three Apex/Eclipse surfaces at once --- the strongest demonstration of the suite working together.}
  \item \sa{Customer incident comms over local networks, POPIA-safe.}
  \item \effort{Medium--High. Status page is light; the incident workflow and integrations are the work.}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Eclipse Voice --- customer feedback portal}
Collect feature requests and bug reports, triage them into Apex.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \hook{$\rightarrow$ Tasks/Projects.} Feedback becomes Apex tasks; status flows back to the requester automatically.
  \item \fit{Closes the classic product loop (request $\rightarrow$ build $\rightarrow$ notify) that PM tools are expected to support.}
  \item \effort{Medium. A public portal/widget plus a triage-to-task bridge.}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Git/CI insights agent}
A lightweight agent that feeds real commit, PR and review data into Apex.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \hook{$\rightarrow$ Contribution Intelligence.} Powers the heatmap, leaderboard and velocity charts with real activity instead of relying on third-party API limits.
  \item \fit{Makes Apex's headline analytics genuinely data-driven.}
  \item \effort{Medium. Webhook/poller + ingestion and aggregation pipeline.}
\end{itemize}

\section{Tier 2 --- complementary}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Pulse --- async standups.} Collects daily updates, posts them to the Apex activity feed and nudges teammates over WhatsApp via OmniConnect. \emph{Apex hook:} Tasks + Notifications.
  \item \textbf{AI assistant over WhatsApp.} Extends the AI Command Center so people can ask ``what's at risk?'' from chat, with OmniConnect as the transport. \emph{Apex hook:} AI Command Center.
  \item \textbf{Eclipse Pay --- contributor payments.} Pay contributors per milestone via Yoco / Paystack / Ozow, driven by Apex contributions. \emph{Apex hook:} Contributions + Milestones. \emph{SA angle:} local payment rails.
\end{itemize}
